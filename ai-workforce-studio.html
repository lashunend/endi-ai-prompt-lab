04 / Get started

Set up your own AI workforce

  1. Choose an agent from the library or build one from scratch.
  2. Complete the required fields and generate your master instructions.
  3. Copy the instructions and open your own ChatGPT account.
  4. Create a separate Project for the assistant if Projects are available to you. Give it a descriptive name.
  5. Open the Project settings and paste the instructions into its instructions field. If that field is unavailable, start a dedicated conversation and paste the prompt there.
  6. Attach relevant files, such as your portfolio, brand guidelines or course notes. Share only information you are authorized to use.
  7. Ask the assistant to begin its onboarding interview. Confirm its understanding before delegating work.
  8. Use the Task Delegation Studio whenever you want to assign a specific job.
  9. For recurring work, ask ChatGPT to help configure a supported scheduled task, if available, and verify the schedule is actually active.
Never share passwords or secret API keys. Review research and generated deliverables before publishing, sending or making business decisions. Projects organize work; they do not automatically create autonomous agents.
